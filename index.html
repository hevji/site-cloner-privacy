Privacy Policy

For Site Cloner  ·  by hevji  ·  Last updated: May 9, 2025

Short version: Site Cloner does not collect, store, transmit, or share any data about you or your browsing. Everything happens locally on your device.

01 —

What Site Cloner Does

Site Cloner is a Chrome extension that allows you to download the source files of any webpage you are currently viewing — including HTML, CSS, JavaScript, images, and fonts — packaged as a local zip file.

02 —

Data We Collect

We collect nothing. Site Cloner does not collect, log, store, or transmit any personal data, browsing history, or information about the pages you visit.

  • No analytics or telemetry
  • No crash reporting
  • No usage statistics
  • No account or sign-in required
  • No cookies or tracking of any kind
03 —

How It Works

When you click Clone, the extension injects a script into the active tab to collect resource URLs from the page. Those resources are then fetched and assembled into a zip file entirely within your browser. The resulting zip is saved directly to your device via the browser's download mechanism.

No data ever leaves your device to our servers — because we have no servers.

04 —

Permissions Explained

Site Cloner requests the following Chrome permissions:

  • activeTab — to access the page you're currently viewing when you click the extension
  • scripting — to inject a resource-collection script into the active tab
  • downloads — to save the generated zip file to your device
  • host_permissions (<all_urls>) — to fetch resources from any domain the page loads assets from

These permissions are used solely to perform the cloning operation you initiate. They are never used to monitor, record, or transmit your activity.

05 —

Third-Party Services

Site Cloner does not integrate with any third-party services, APIs, or analytics platforms. The only external code bundled with the extension is JSZip (an open-source zip library), which runs entirely in your browser.
